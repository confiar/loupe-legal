LeafLoupe Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 22, 2026 · Developer: Loupe Apps Studio

1. Zero-Login & On-Device Windowsill Data

LeafLoupe requires no user account, email registration, or personal profile. Your saved houseplants, soil-check watering schedules, ambient light (LUX) measurements, and offline pet-toxicity lookups are stored locally on your device.

2. Camera, Ambient Light Sensor & Transient AI Vision Processing

Ambient light readings from your device sensor are processed exclusively on-device in real time and are never recorded or transmitted as personal data. When you initiate an online AI plant or leaf scan, a resized JPEG (<180 KB) along with non-personal plant context (measured lux and pot type) is transmitted over encrypted HTTPS to our edge processor and analyzed transiently by the commercial Google Gemini API solely to identify plant species and visible foliage stress. Photos are never sold, never linked to personal identity, and never used to train foundation models.

3. Anonymous Quota Protection & In-App Purchases

LeafLoupe uses a random anonymous device identifier (with Apple App Attest / Google Play Integrity) to enforce free AI scan credits and uses RevenueCat to validate Google Play and Apple App Store subscriptions. LeafLoupe contains zero third-party advertising SDKs and does not track users across apps or websites.

4. Botanical & Pet Safety Disclaimer & Contact

LeafLoupe is an independent horticultural care and plant reference utility. It is not a veterinary or medical diagnostic service; if a pet or person ingests any plant material, contact a licensed veterinarian or poison control center immediately.

5. Data Retention & Deletion Instructions

All saved windowsill plants, photos, and care logs reside locally on your device and can be deleted instantly inside the app or by uninstalling LeafLoupe. Transient scan images are discarded immediately after AI inference and are never stored on our servers. To request deletion of your anonymous device quota record, email confiar89@gmail.com with subject LeafLoupe Data Deletion and we will purge any matching record within 7 days.
