WaxLoupe Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 21, 2026 · Developer: Loupe Apps Studio

1. Zero-Account & Local-First Design

WaxLoupe does not require user registration, email sign-up, or personal accounts. Your vinyl crate inventory, condition grades, and personal notes are stored locally on your device in an SQLite database.

2. Camera & Transient AI Vision Processing

When you scan a vinyl album sleeve, center label, or deadwax matrix runout using online AI identification, a compressed image (<180 KB) is transmitted securely over HTTPS to our edge function and processed transiently via the commercial Google Gemini API solely to extract visible release metadata. Submitted images are not stored permanently on our servers, are not linked to personal identity, and are not used to train foundation AI models.

3. Anonymous Device Quota & Subscription Billing

To enforce free-scan quotas and verify Pro subscriptions without requiring a user login, WaxLoupe generates a random, non-tracking anonymous device identifier stored in your device Keychain/Keystore and uses Apple App Store / Google Play Store in-app billing via RevenueCat. No advertising SDKs or cross-app tracking (IDFA) are used.

4. Your Rights & Contact

You may delete all local crate data at any time by deleting items in the app or uninstalling WaxLoupe. For privacy inquiries or anonymous quota reset requests, contact: confiar89@gmail.com.
