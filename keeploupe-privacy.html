KeepLoupe Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 28, 2026 · Developer: Loupe Apps Studio

1. Zero-Bank, Zero-Login & Local-First Document Vault

KeepLoupe never connects to bank accounts, credit cards, Plaid, or email accounts via OAuth or IMAP, and requires no user account. Your saved receipt images, serial numbers, return deadlines, warranty expiration dates, free-trial reminders, and exported PDF/CSV files are stored locally on your device.

2. Optional On-Device Notification Auto-Capture (Android)

On Android, KeepLoupe offers an optional, opt-in Notification Auto-Capture feature (disabled by default). Only if you explicitly agree to our in-app disclosure and grant Android Notification Access does KeepLoupe access incoming notifications—including while the app is closed or in the background—from supported store, payment, and receipt email apps (such as Google Play, Gmail, Samsung Email, Outlook, Naver Mail, Samsung Pay, Google Wallet, KakaoPay, Naver Pay, Toss, Coupang, Amazon, and eBay) solely to detect purchase amounts and trial or refund deadlines and create local reminders. Personal chat, SMS, social media, and Android Work Profile notifications are excluded at the package/profile level and never read. Matched notifications are temporarily queued in a private, no-backup on-device file (excluded from Android Auto Backup and device-to-device transfer), parsed 100% on-device, and purged immediately after ingestion or when you disable the feature or revoke Notification Access. Notification text is never uploaded to our servers or shared with any third party.

3. Camera & Transient AI Receipt OCR Processing

When you run an online AI receipt, warranty card, or trial screenshot scan, a compressed JPEG image is sent over encrypted HTTPS to our edge processor and analyzed transiently by the commercial Google Gemini API solely to extract merchant name, purchase date, total amount, and serial/order text. Images are processed ephemerally in memory, never stored in cloud buckets, never sold, and never linked to personal identity.

4. Anonymous Quota Protection & In-App Purchases

KeepLoupe uses a random anonymous device identifier (with Apple App Attest / Google Play Integrity) to manage free AI scan credits and uses RevenueCat to validate Google Play and Apple App Store purchases. KeepLoupe contains zero third-party advertising trackers.

5. Independent Organizer Disclaimer & Contact

KeepLoupe is a personal document organizer and deadline reminder tool, not a financial institution, accounting firm, or legal advisor. Always verify retailer return policies and manufacturer warranty terms directly on your original receipt.

6. Data Retention & Deletion Instructions

All saved receipts, warranty notes, serial numbers, and deadlines reside locally on your device and can be deleted at any time inside KeepLoupe or by uninstalling the app. Transient OCR scan images are discarded immediately after parsing and are never stored on our servers. To request deletion of your anonymous device quota record, email confiar89@gmail.com with subject KeepLoupe Data Deletion and we will purge any matching record within 7 days.
