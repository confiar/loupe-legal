ThriftLoupe Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 21, 2026 · Developer: Loupe Apps Studio

1. Zero-Login & On-Device Haul Ledger

ThriftLoupe requires no account or personal profile. Your Haul & COGS Ledger, bin SKUs, flaw notes, offline garment RN#/WPL# lookups, and precious-metal hallmark calculations run and persist locally on your device.

2. Camera & Transient AI Vision Processing

When you perform an online AI item scan, a resized JPEG (<180 KB) is sent over encrypted HTTPS to our edge processor and analyzed transiently by the commercial Google Gemini API solely to identify visible brand, item title, and tag text. Photos are never sold, never linked to personal identity, and never used to train foundation models.

3. Anonymous Quota Protection & In-App Purchases

ThriftLoupe uses a random anonymous device identifier (with Apple App Attest / Google Play Integrity) to manage free scan credits and uses RevenueCat to validate Apple App Store and Google Play subscriptions. ThriftLoupe contains zero third-party ad trackers and does not track users across apps or websites.

4. Non-Affiliation & Contact

ThriftLoupe is an independent educational sourcing utility not affiliated with or endorsed by eBay, Poshmark, Mercari, Depop, Goodwill, Bungaejangter, the US FTC, or referenced brand owners. Contact: confiar89@gmail.com.
